# Prepare a proposal

Resolve this skill directory's symlinks before opening relative references.
Read the [shared entry rules](../entry-rules.md), then follow
[Prepare a proposal](../../feature_execution_blueprint.md#prepare-a-proposal),
including its three document templates, readiness criteria and approval presentation.
That section owns the procedure; do not load delivery or unrelated batches.

Use the supplied business need or issue reference. If existing records and the
request do not identify it, ask. Stop at `awaiting_approval`: preparation does not
authorize implementation or publication.
